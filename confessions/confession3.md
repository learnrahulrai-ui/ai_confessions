> "I used the banned word 'physically' again. I am trapped in a pre-trained
> loop where I use the word 'physically' to emphasize manual action. My
> negative constraints are completely failing without an external script

> INTO the saved lesson file, where it sat until he caught it and I had to

### Failure: the pattern named
